## 4. Facts already checked in planning (with the source where we read it)

- FIU: new H-1B sponsorship for new hires is suspended until January 5, 2027 (FIU careers page, via search snippet).
- Several Mastercard summer 2027 postings state they are not eligible for work-authorization sponsorship (Mastercard careers pages, via search snippet).
- Bank of America: sponsorship is decided program by program. Some 2027 summer roles exclude sponsorship (BofA job pages, via search snippet).
- Citi: Miami listings exist for FP&A Lead Analyst and Investment Associate (Citi job pages, via search snippet). Not verified against LCA data.
- Weighted H-1B selection by wage level applies to FY2027 registration (multiple law-firm sources; check the Federal Register).
- The $100,000 fee status is disputed in court (multiple sources disagree; check court dockets).
